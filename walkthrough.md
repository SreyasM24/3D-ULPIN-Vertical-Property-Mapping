## 1. System Integration Architecture

The platform strictly enforces the foundational cadastral boundary:

```
REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE (Imagery, LiDAR, Survey Metadata, Floor Plans)
        ↓
AI / EXTRACTION (BuildingDetector U-Net, HeightEstimator MLP)
        ↓
EVIDENCE + PROVENANCE (Auditable Source Tags, Uncertainty ±m, Confidence)
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC CADASTRAL CONSTRUCTION (Building, FloorLevel strata, VerticalUnit)
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC VALIDATION (Geometry, Hierarchy, 3D Spatial Clash, Strata)
        ↓
PROTOTYPE 3D ULPIN (Deterministic Luhn mod-36 Dual Checksum)
        ↓
3D DIGITAL TWIN (Volumetric Model & 3D GeoJSON FeatureCollection)
        ↓
TEMPORAL 3D CHANGE INTELLIGENCE (Advisory Multi-Epoch Auditing)
```

**Core Principle**: AI models propose candidate geometry and evidence. Authoritative cadastral truth is constructed solely by deterministic spatial engines and verified through 102+ cadastral validation rules. AI inference never directly mutates or creates legal cadastral truth.

## 3. Strict Provenance Taxonomy

Every derived entity in the platform carries an auditable provenance label that cannot be confused:

### 3.1 Building Footprint
- `AI_ONNX_INFERENCE`: Generated by Lightweight U-Net ONNX runtime inference from RGB imagery.
- `PASS_THROUGH_OBSERVED`: Ingested directly from registered cadastral boundary polygons.
- `DETERMINISTIC_FALLBACK`: Unconfigured/empty fallback when no imagery or geometry is available.

### 3.2 Height Estimation
- `OBSERVED_ELEVATION_EVIDENCE`: Derived from high-fidelity DSM-DTM raster difference or airborne LiDAR point cloud returns.
- `OBSERVED_SURVEY_METADATA`: Extracted from municipal survey or registered architectural documentation.
- `AI_REGRESSION`: Inferred by `HeightRegressorMLP` ONNX model from geometric footprint features.
- `DETERMINISTIC_CASCADE`: Estimated parametrically from floor counts ($3.8\text{m} + (N-1) \times 3.0\text{m}$).
- `UNRESOLVED`: Insufficient data to determine building height.

### 3.3 Floor Count & Vertical Strata
- `EXPLICIT_FLOOR_COUNT`: Taken directly from verified survey metadata.
- `ARCHITECTURAL_FLOOR_PLAN`: Parsed from architectural CAD / BIM drawing level definitions.
- `OBSERVED_HEIGHT_DECOMPOSITION`: Parametrically decomposed from LiDAR or DSM-DTM elevation observations.
- `AI_HEIGHT_DECOMPOSITION`: Decomposed from neural network height regression; **requires surveyor review**.
- `DETERMINISTIC_BASELINE`: Standard single-storey default assumption; **requires surveyor review**.
- `UNRESOLVED`: Insufficient height and floor count data.

### 3.4 Temporal 3D Change
- `OBSERVED_CHANGE`: Physical survey or LiDAR multi-epoch observation.
- `AI_ESTIMATED_CHANGE`: Machine-learning inference proposal; **strictly flags human review**.
- `DETERMINISTIC_CHANGE`: Geometric diff between structured survey epochs.
- `TEST_FIXTURE_CHANGE`: Controlled test simulation fixture.

## 4. Confidence Semantics Disambiguation

The platform strictly isolates four distinct confidence and quality metrics:

1. **AI Model Confidence** ($\in [0.0, 1.0]$):
   - Measure of neural network prediction certainty (e.g. sigmoid activation density or regression variance).
   - *Never indicates legal cadastral validity.*
2. **Deterministic Technical Quality** (Uncertainty $\pm\text{m}$):
   - Sensor resolution and empirical error bounds (e.g. $\pm 0.15\text{m}$ for LiDAR, $\pm 2.32\text{m}$ for ML height regression).
   - *Reflects engineering tolerances, not title accuracy.*
3. **Cadastral Validation Score** (0–100 pts, Grades A–D):
   - Evaluated by `CadastralQualityScorer` across 6 dimensions: Geometry Validity (25), CRS (15), Hierarchy (20), Strata (15), Clash Freedom (15), and Attribute Completeness (10).
   - *Algorithmic data quality metric; does not constitute government title guarantee.*
4. **Temporal Technical Change Score** (0.00–1.00, Severity: `NONE` to `CRITICAL`):
   - Multi-metric composite difference score: IoU displacement (35%), height delta (35%), floor delta (20%), unit delta (10%).
   - *Advisory change detection metric; does not predict structural failure or collapse.*
